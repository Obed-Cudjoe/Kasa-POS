# Kasa POS launch kit

**Niche assumption:** independent neighborhood grocery and convenience shops in Accra/Ghana. This is inferred from the current demo catalogue; replace it if the target niche changes.

## Deliverables

- `onboarding-guide.md` — staging-first owner setup checklist.
- `cashier-training-video-script.md` — short checkout/refund training storyboard.
- `pricing-and-monetization.md` — affordability hypothesis and launch caveats.
- `landing-page.html` — responsive, self-contained landing-page draft.
- `support-and-feedback.md`, `feedback-intake-template.md`, `90-day-growth-plan.md` — support and discovery drafts.

## Readiness status

This is a planning and content package, not a completed commercial launch. The public Kasa POS Worker remains demo-only until a real D1 resource is created/bound, migrations and secrets are configured, and staging tests pass. The D1 staging location is US jurisdiction; Ghana privacy and transfer implications require review before real data.

The source now includes D1-backed account routes, owner self-registration, owner-issued staff invitations, barcode capture/checkout, and receipt-name settings. Those code paths have not yet been validated against a remote D1 database, Resend account, physical barcode reader, or store printer. A high-priority digital-payment inventory oversell risk remains under review. Do not publish claims of GRA/E-VAT certification, guaranteed tax compliance, live-payment readiness, proven multi-location support or production security approval.

Pricing is hypothetical, subscription enforcement is not implemented, and the support contact on the landing-page draft is still a placeholder. Use this kit for discovery and controlled synthetic-data staging only until the relevant launch gates are closed.
