Questions shop owners ask

Good to know before you start.

Does Kasa POS work when the internet drops?

The current app supports local cash-sale workflows and queues changes for sync. First-time setup/sign-in needs internet. Digital payment requests require connectivity and verified provider confirmation.

Are the tax rates already approved?

No. A shop owner must use an accountant/GRA-adviser-approved configuration. Demo rates are examples only; Kasa POS does not claim GRA/E-VAT certification.

Can I accept card and mobile money?

Not in the current setup: checkout is cash-only and no payment gateway is configured. Digital payments would require a separate product decision, merchant onboarding and end-to-end staging tests.

Can I run multiple shops from one account?

Multi-location access is not ready. The current launch configuration is one store per deployment; cross-shop isolation and shared staff access need more work.
